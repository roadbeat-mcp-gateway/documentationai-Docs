title: Interactive Cards
description: MCP Apps cards for the Compass daily review, your bookmarks and Studio publishing — what they show, what you can do in them, and how they stay safe.

In clients that support **MCP Apps** (`io.modelcontextprotocol/ui`), some tool results appear as an interactive card in the conversation instead of plain text. Besides the ["why am I seeing this?" card](/tools/why-card), three cards cover everyday tasks:

| Card | Shown for | What you can do in it |
|------|-----------|-----------------------|
| **Daily review** | [`compass_review_today`](/tools/compass#compass_review_today) | See what is new for each of your goals; **Bookmark** an item with one click |
| **Bookmarks** | [`cd_list_bookmarks`](/tools/personal-data#cd_list_bookmarks) | Filter by collection, put a bookmark into a collection, create a new collection |
| **Publish status** | [`studio_publish_content`, `studio_unpublish_content`, `studio_get_publish_status`](/tools/studio#publishing) | See the item's status and where its teaser is on each Discovery Node; the card checks again while a node is still pending |

## Daily review

For every active goal, highest priority first: the new items with publisher, type and date, a short description, and a **Bookmark** button. A note says when more items are available. Links open through your client.

## Bookmarks

Your bookmarks with a row of collection chips (**All** and each collection with its count) to filter. Each bookmark has an **Add to collection…** menu; **New collection** creates one. Bookmarks whose teaser is gone are marked *no longer available*.

## Publish status

The item's title, status, type, locale and version, then one row per Discovery Node: *published*, *pending*, *unpublished* or *failed*, the teaser id, since when, and the node's last error. After a publish the card asks for the node status itself and checks again every few seconds (up to five times) while a node is still pending; **Check again** refreshes it any time.

## Actions run through your client

When you click a button in a card, the card asks your client to call the corresponding gateway tool — `cd_add_bookmark`, `cd_add_to_collection`, `cd_create_collection` or `studio_get_publish_status`. Everything that applies when the assistant calls a tool applies here too: your **consent** for the app (the first bookmark may ask you), **rate limits** and the **audit log**. If an action is refused, the card shows the reason next to the button.

The bookmark tools are part of the core `/mcp` endpoint, so these cards work there as well as on `/mcp/cd`, `/mcp/goals` and `/mcp/all`. The publish status card is available on `/mcp/studio` and `/mcp/all`.

## Built to be safe

- Each card is one self-contained HTML file served as a `ui://roadbeat/…` resource (`text/html;profile=mcp-app`). It loads nothing from other websites, so it works under the strictest content security policy a client applies, and it needs no form or pop-up permissions.
- Publisher and user text is rendered strictly as text — titles or descriptions can never inject markup or scripts.
- The cards follow your client's light or dark theme and work with the keyboard and a screen reader (labelled buttons and menus, announced status messages).

## Without MCP Apps

Clients without the extension get the same results as structured data and text. The assistant can do the same actions by calling the tools when you ask it to.

  The cards are a **beta**. They were tested in a browser with a simulated MCP Apps host (light and dark, every action); verification in Claude and ChatGPT is still pending.
</Callout>
