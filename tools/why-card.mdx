Transparency is a core roadbeat promise: you can always ask **why** something was shown to you. Two tools answer that question, and in clients that support **MCP Apps** (`io.modelcontextprotocol/ui`) the answer appears as an interactive card right in the conversation.

| Tool | Explains |
|------|----------|
| [`discovery_explain_result`](/tools/discovery#discovery_explain_result) | Why a teaser matches a **search**: text match with score and highlights, and every filter compared with the teaser's actual value |
| [`compass_explain_match`](/tools/compass#compass_explain_match) | Why a teaser matches one of **your goals**: each content query of the goal, matched or not, with the field-by-field comparison |

## What the card shows

- A headline verdict: **Matches your search**, **Partly matches your search**, **Matches your goal** or **Does not match your goal**.
- **Text** — for searches: whether the query matched, the relevance score and highlighted passages.
- **Filters** — for searches: each filter you used, the expected and actual value, and a ✓ or ✗.
- **Goal queries** — for goals: *This query matched* / *This query did not match*, with the content type, relevance and compared fields.
- **Publisher** — the publisher's name, whether it is verified, and its trust level.

The card follows your client's light or dark theme.

## Built to be safe

- The card is one self-contained HTML file served as the resource `ui://roadbeat/why-card.html` (`text/html;profile=mcp-app`). It loads nothing from other websites, so it works under the strictest content security policy a client applies.
- It renders publisher text strictly as text — titles or highlights can never inject markup or scripts.
- It only displays the tool's result; it does not call back into roadbeat.

## Without MCP Apps

Clients without the extension simply get the same explanation as structured data and text, and the assistant explains it in words. Nothing is lost except the visual layout.
